Services

One studio, from site to handover.

Four services, delivered by the same small team, so the idea you approve is the building you get.

What wedesign

01

Architecture

New buildings from first sketch to handover, with full design and site supervision. We start on site, study the light through a full day, then draw the building around it.

What's included
  • Site and daylight study
  • Concept design and planning applications
  • Detailed design and specification
  • Tender support and site supervision
Seen in

Tidewater House and Vault Gallery.

02

Interiors

Joinery, lighting and finishes designed together with the building so rooms feel complete from the day you move in.

What's included
  • Space planning and room layouts
  • Bespoke joinery and fittings
  • Lighting design
  • Material, colour and furniture schedules
Seen in

Reading Rooms, where each tower is fitted out as its own quiet room.

03

Restoration

Careful repair and reuse of older buildings, with new work that is clearly of its time and easy to tell apart from the original.

What's included
  • Building survey and condition report
  • Heritage and materials appraisal
  • Structural repair and conservation
  • Adaptive reuse and new additions
Seen in

Granary Studios, a 1920s grain store turned into artist workspaces.

04

Masterplanning

Neighbourhood and campus plans that connect streets, open space and buildings, and can be built in sensible stages.

What's included
  • Site and context analysis
  • Street, block and open space layout
  • Phasing and delivery strategy
  • Community consultation and design codes
Seen in

Campus and neighbourhood plans for schools, councils and developers.
